Rama is one of the most revered figures in Hindu tradition, celebrated as the seventh avatar of Lord Vishnu. He is the central character of the ancient Indian epic, the Ramayana, written by the sage Valmiki.

Born as the prince of Ayodhya to King Dasharatha and Queen Kausalya, Rama is regarded as the embodiment of dharma (righteousness), virtue, and ideal conduct. His life story symbolizes the triumph of good over evil.

Key highlights of Rama's life:
- Married to Sita, the princess of Mithila, after lifting and breaking the divine bow of Shiva.
- Exiled to the forest for 14 years to honor his father's promise to Queen Kaikeyi.
- Accompanied by his loyal wife Sita and devoted brother Lakshmana during the exile.
- Battled and defeated the demon king Ravana of Lanka, who had abducted Sita.
- Aided by Hanuman, the mighty vanara devotee, and an army of vanaras.
- Returned to Ayodhya to be crowned king, marking the festival of Diwali.

Rama is worshipped as Maryada Purushottama — the ideal man — and his story continues to inspire millions across generations with its timeless lessons of duty, devotion, and moral courage.

Jai Shri Ram!
