Rama

Rama is a legendary figure revered in Hindu tradition as the seventh avatar of Vishnu and the central hero of the ancient Sanskrit epic, the Ramayana, composed by the sage Valmiki. Born as the eldest prince of Ayodhya to King Dasharatha and Queen Kausalya, Rama is celebrated as the embodiment of dharma — righteousness, virtue, and duty.

His life story follows his exile to the forest for fourteen years, the abduction of his devoted wife Sita by the demon king Ravana, his alliance with the vanara king Sugriva and the loyal Hanuman, and the eventual battle in Lanka where good triumphs over evil. His return to Ayodhya is celebrated annually as Diwali, the festival of lights.

Rama is admired not just as a warrior, but as an ideal son, husband, brother, and king — a symbol of integrity, compassion, and unwavering moral resolve.

"Jai Shri Ram"
